# Squared Circle Universe: Wrestling Booking Simulation
Take the reigns of a wrestling company with this simulation app!

## About This App
Squared Circle Universe simulates the week to week programming of a wrestling company, allowing you to manage a roster of wrestlers, book them in matches on shows, and culminate story lines in marquee pay-per-view events. 

This app was heavily inspired by WWE 2K's Universe Mode. This beloved mode has always suffered due to not leveraging the full power of the technology of today. Squared Circle Universe looks to bring the idea of a "Universe Mode" into modern tech, allowing you to more easily edit, trade and and create every aspect of your wrestling company. 

## Planned Features:
- Create your own wrestling company
- Build and manage your roster using simple text and CSV files
- Trade your favorite wrestlers or companies with friends
- Book and simulate matches and storylines
- Run weekly shows that culminate in special events
- Create your own championships and divisions with NO limits
- Strictly *not* a GM mode tool -- this is purely for booking, not contracts
